# The tea is worth less than the storyboard said

**Decided 6 September 2026.**

## The problem

The demo's e-SRG was valued at Rp 600,000,000 for 8,000 kg of made tea — an
implied **Rp 75,000/kg**.

The trade price list in the Indonesia Tea Board deck (slide 55) is not an
illustration. It puts bulk made tea, free on truck at the warehouse, at
**Rp 11,000–30,000/kg**.

So the demo was carrying a figure 2.5 to 5 times the market. That is not a
cosmetic error. Everything downstream is derived from the appraised value:

```
ceiling      = appraised value × policy LTV
Senior       = 270/420 of ceiling
Junior       = 120/420 of ceiling
LTV headline = "70%"
```

At Rp 25,000/kg, 8,000 kg is worth Rp 200,000,000. A Rp 420,000,000 facility
against it is not 70% LTV — it is 210%, and the headline claim inverts.

A judge who knows the sector would find this in one question.

## What was considered

**Lower the facility to match 8,000 kg.** Honest, but every financing figure
moves, and the deployed note's Senior and Junior partitions would have to be
reissued.

**Call it specialty tea.** Specialty does fetch far more. But the entire premise
is that Indonesia exports 88% bulk at a discount to benchmark; making the demo
lot specialty argues against the project's own thesis.

**Raise the quantity, keep the value.** Chosen.

## Decision

```
before    8,000 kg × Rp 75,000  = Rp 600,000,000
after    24,000 kg × Rp 25,000  = Rp 600,000,000
```

Rp 25,000/kg sits inside the Tea Board band, above the middle — defensible for
good-grade BOP without reaching for the top of the range.

24 tonnes of made tea is an ordinary warehouse lot. National production is
roughly 118,895 t per year.

Every financing figure is unchanged: ceiling Rp 420,000,000, LTV 70%, Senior
Rp 270,000,000, Junior Rp 120,000,000, Rp 30,000,000 unissued. Only the quantity
moved, so nothing on-chain had to be reissued.

The eight supplier intakes scale by exactly 3 and stay whole numbers.

## What is still assumed

The Rp 25,000/kg figure is a point chosen inside a published band, not a quote
for this lot. There is no real e-SRG behind it — `docs/STATUS.md` lists the
receipt as permanently simulated. The number is defensible, not sourced.

Source: Indonesia Tea Board deck, slide 55, trade price list. Read 5 September
2026.
